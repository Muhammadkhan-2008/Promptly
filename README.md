# Promptly 🚀

**Promptly** is an advanced AI-powered prompt generator designed to help you craft the perfect prompts for your AI models, whether you're working with text, images, or videos. 

Often, we have an idea in our mind but struggle to find the exact words to explain it to an AI. Promptly solves this problem by taking your basic ideas or media files and transforming them into highly detailed, optimized, and effective prompts.

## ✨ Features

*   **📝 Text-to-Prompt (Prompt Expansion):** 
    Provide a simple, short description of what you want (e.g., "a futuristic city"), and Promptly will use AI to expand it into a comprehensive, highly-detailed prompt ready to be fed into tools like Midjourney, ChatGPT, or Stable Diffusion.
*   **🖼️ Image-to-Prompt (Reverse Prompting):** 
    Upload an image, and Promptly will analyze the visual elements, style, and composition to generate the exact prompt needed to recreate that image or something similar.
*   **🎥 Video-to-Prompt:** 
    Extract the core concepts, actions, and aesthetic styles from a video clip and translate them into a descriptive prompt format.

## 🎯 How it Works

1.  **Input:** Feed Promptly with a basic text idea, an image, or a short video clip.
2.  **AI Analysis:** The underlying AI model analyzes your input, understanding the context, style, and missing details.
3.  **Generation:** Promptly outputs a rich, structured, and highly effective prompt that you can instantly copy and use.
